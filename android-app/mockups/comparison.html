Prime versus maximal ideals

Compare your proof

Prove that a maximal ideal in a commutative ring with identity is prime.

Sample draft

Since M is maximal, the quotient R/M is a field. So M is prime.

Model solution

Since M is maximal, R/M is a field. If xy = 0 in this field and x is nonzero, multiplying by its inverse gives y = 0. Thus R/M is an integral domain, so M is prime.
